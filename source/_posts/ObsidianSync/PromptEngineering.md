### :rocket: Get Start With Prompt Engineering
`LLMs`: Large Language Models

Totally , it can be seen as two types of LLMs:
`Base LLM` *Predicts next word, based on text training data.*

& 

`Instruction Tuned LLM` *Tries to follow instructions, Fine-tune on instructions and good attempts at following those instructions.*



### 🍏 Principles * should follow when Prompting
- 1️⃣ Write clear and specific instructions
        - Tactic 1 : Use delimiters like `Triple quotes` , `Triple backticks` , `Triple dashes` , `Angle brackets` , `XML tags` ...
        - Tactic 2 : Ask for structured output like `HTML` , `JSON` ...
        - Tactic 3 : Check whether conditions are satisfied. Check assumptions required to do the task.
        - Tactic 4 : Few-shot prompting : `Give successful examples of completing tasks` then ask model to perform the task.
